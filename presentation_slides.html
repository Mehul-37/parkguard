PARKGUARD

A Tamper-Proof Digital Twin for Municipal Revenue Assurance

Team IGNIS: Mehul | Aavya | Harsh | Rachit | Ayush

PROBLEM STATEMENT

The "Black Hole" of Municipal Parking Revenue

🔴 Revenue "Ghosting"

Cash is collected by contractors on the ground but never recorded in the central system, leading to massive financial leakage.

🔴 Opaque Operations

Municipal corporations have zero real-time visibility into ground-level occupancy, relying on outdated manual reports.

🔴 Audit Failure

Manual paper-based logbooks are easily forged, lost, or manipulated, making post-facto auditing legally impossible.

🔴 Space Inefficiency

Without live data, enforcement officers are reactive, not proactive, leading to congestion and poor asset utilization.

Key Insight: The problem isn't just "parking"—it's the lack of a verifiable link between physical usage and digital payment.

THE PARKGUARD ECOSYSTEM

Bridging the Physical and Digital Worlds

1. Live "Digital Twin"

A dynamic, visual replica of the parking lot that reflects real-time ground reality. Administrators can see occupancy slot-by-slot instantly.

2. I.o.T. Integration

Hardware-agnostic sensor integration detects physical vehicle presence (Ultrasonic/Camera) independent of the payment system.

3. Cryptographic Ledger

Every booking, entry, and exit is hashed on a blockchain implementation, creating an immutable legal record that cannot be edited.

4. Automated Audit

The system automatically flags discrepancies between Physical Presence (Sensor) and Digital Record (Payment).

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The Trust Loop: ensuring data integrity at every step.

Step 1: Physical Layer (Input)

  • IoT Sensors detect car.
  • Cameras read License Plate.
  • Driver App generates Request.
➜

Step 2: Verification Layer (Process)

  • Backend (FastAPI): Validates request.
  • Logic Check: Is Slot Empty? Is Payment Valid?
  • Space Allocation: Assigns optimal slot.
➜

Step 3: Audit Layer (Storage)

  • Blockchain Ledger: Hashes the transaction event.
  • Auditor Dashboard: Verifies the chain hash.
  • Status: Valid/Tampered Alert.
Decentralized verification ensures that no single actor—contractor, admin, or user—can alter the history.

KEY FEATURES & INNOVATION

Why ParkGuard is a Government-Grade Solution

✅ Automated Anomaly Detection

The system autonomously compares sensor data against payment records. If a car is physically parked but acts "Digital Ghost" (unpaid), the slot turns RED and alerts authorities.

✅ Immutable Audit Trails

Utilizes SHA-256 Merkle Chaining. Records cannot be altered, deleted, or backdated. One-click "Chain Verification" proves data integrity for legal admissibility.

✅ Dynamic Infrastructure

Map Editor & Digital Twin: Fully customizable mapping tools allow administrators to update parking layouts, price zones, and sensor positions on the fly.

✅ Smart Yield Optimization

A* Pathfinding Algorithms guide drivers to the nearest open slot, maximizing turnover, reducing fuel emissions, and increasing revenue per square foot.
